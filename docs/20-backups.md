## The catch
The single existing backup (Oct 29, 12:04 PM, Automatic, 7 KB - matching the Activity Log's
automatic-backup entry) has its "Download" and "Restore" buttons DISABLED, marked
"Temporarily unavailable". In the observed build, a backup exists that the school cannot
download or restore. Treat the feature as present-but-inert until those actions work.
